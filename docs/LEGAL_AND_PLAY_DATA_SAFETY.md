# Legal and Play Data Safety Checklist

This is an engineering checklist for the current LapKart web and Android app. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed by the business owner or counsel before store/payment review.

## Public policy URLs

- Privacy policy: `https://www.lapkart.store/privacy`
- Terms and conditions: `https://www.lapkart.store/terms`
- Contact and support: `https://www.lapkart.store/contact`
- Support and complaints: `https://www.lapkart.store/grievances`
- Shipping policy: `https://www.lapkart.store/shipping-policy`
- Return policy: `https://www.lapkart.store/returns-policy`
- Cancellation and refund policy: `https://www.lapkart.store/cancellation-refunds`

## Merchant/reseller model

- Customer-facing model: LapKart sells to the customer as the merchant/reseller of record.
- Procurement model: suppliers or procurement partners may bill LapKart internally for goods supplied to LapKart.
- Customer documents and support should show LapKart as the seller. Do not show supplier names, supplier GST bills, supplier settlements, or third-party commercial terms in customer-facing flows.

## Data collected by current app behavior

- Account and identity: user id, email, phone, name, auth session identifiers.
- Contact and delivery: addresses, pincode, delivery phone, optional map-selected coordinates.
- Orders and commerce: cart contents on device, order lines, totals, order receipt/accounting data, payment references, refund status, return status, delivery status.
- Support: complaint category, subject, description, linked order id, resolution notes, return evidence submitted by the user.
- Device/app operation: cookie or consent choices, service-worker caches of public catalog data, app diagnostics generated by browser/native runtime.
- Push notification token: only if Android push registration is granted by the user and registration succeeds.
- Camera/gallery files: only when the user chooses an image for a supported workflow.
- Location: only when the user grants browser/native location permission for delivery map/address help.

## Data not stored by LapKart

- Card PIN, UPI PIN, banking password, and full payment credentials.
- Private checkout, orders, profile, admin, or payment pages in the offline service-worker cache.

## Processors and services to declare

- Supabase: authentication, database, storage/API infrastructure.
- Razorpay and selected payment app/provider: online payment processing and payment references.
- Ola Maps: map/address/location support when used.
- Google Analytics: web analytics only when consent permits; native app sessions currently skip analytics installation.
- Manual courier/support operations: order delivery, proof, returns, refunds, and complaint handling.

## Google Play Data Safety notes

- The app uses a WebView controlled by LapKart, so data collected through the WebView must be declared.
- Declare encryption in transit because the app uses HTTPS/TLS for network calls.
- Declare a deletion request mechanism: `support@lapkart.store` and `/grievances` with the Privacy / data category.
- Declare location as optional if it is only requested for delivery map/address help.
- Declare camera/photos as optional if only requested when the user chooses to upload evidence or an image.
- Declare financial information/order history where Play asks about purchases, payment info, and transaction data. LapKart receives payment references and order history, not full card/UPI credentials.
- Keep the Play Console declaration updated whenever SDKs, analytics, push notifications, permissions, payment providers, or data handling change.

## Owner-supplied details still required

Do not invent these in code. Add them to the contact/terms pages when the owner confirms them:

- Registered business or legal entity name.
- Principal place of business / registered address.
- GSTIN or other statutory identifiers only if registration becomes applicable and the owner confirms they should be published.
- Named grievance/privacy contact if different from `support@lapkart.store`.
- Final return pickup address or inspection address, if required for public display.
